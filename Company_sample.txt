14. RETURN AND REFUND POLICY
    At ACME Corporation, we want you to be completely satisfied with your
    purchase. Our return and refund policy is designed to be fair and
    straightforward:

    Eligibility:
    - Returns are accepted within 30 days of the original purchase date.
    - The product must be in its original condition with all original
      packaging, accessories, and documentation included.
    - Digital products (software licenses, subscriptions) may be refunded
      within 14 days of purchase if the product has not been activated or
      used extensively.
    - Custom-developed solutions are non-refundable once development work
      has commenced, unless there is a documented defect.

    Refund Process:
    - To initiate a return or refund, contact our customer support team
      via the chat widget, email at support@acmecorp.com, or call
      1-800-ACME-HELP.
    - Provide your Order ID, name, and reason for the return.
    - Our AI assistant can help you create a refund ticket instantly.
    - Once your return is received and inspected, we will process your
      refund within 5-7 business days.
    - Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

    Exceptions:
    - Products damaged due to misuse or unauthorized modifications are
      not eligible for return.
    - Clearance items and promotional bundles are final sale.
    - Enterprise contracts have separate return terms outlined in the
      service agreement.

    Exchanges:
    - We offer free exchanges for the same product (different tier/plan)
      within 30 days.
    - Upgrades can be processed with a prorated price difference.

19. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

    Q: How do I track my order?
    A: You can track your order by providing your Order ID (format: ORD-XXXX)
       to our AI assistant or by visiting our order tracking page. You will
       also receive tracking updates via email.

    Q: Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?
    A: Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placement.
       After that, the order enters processing and cannot be changed.
       Contact support immediately if you need to make changes.

    Q: What if my product arrives damaged?
    A: If your product arrives damaged, please contact us within 48 hours
       of delivery with photos of the damage. We will arrange a free
       replacement or full refund. Our AI assistant can create a ticket
       for you right away.

    Q: Do you offer volume discounts?
    A: Yes! We offer tiered pricing for bulk purchases:
       - 5-9 licenses: 10% discount
       - 10-24 licenses: 15% discount
       - 25-49 licenses: 20% discount
       - 50+ licenses: Contact sales for custom pricing

    Q: How do I upgrade my subscription plan?
    A: You can upgrade your plan at any time through your account dashboard
       or by contacting our support team. The price difference will be
       prorated for the remaining billing period.

    Q: What is your uptime guarantee?
    A: We guarantee 99.9% uptime for all our cloud-hosted services. In the
       rare event of downtime exceeding our SLA, we provide service credits
       as outlined in your service agreement.

    Q: Do you offer a free trial?
    A: Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial for most of our software products.
       No credit card required. You can start your trial through our website
       or by asking our AI assistant.

    Q: How do I reset my password?
    A: Visit our login page and click "Forgot Password." You'll receive a
       password reset link via email within 5 minutes. If you don't receive
       it, check your spam folder or contact support.

    Q: Can I get a demo before purchasing?
    A: Absolutely! We offer personalized product demos for all our solutions.
       Schedule a demo through our website or ask our AI assistant to help
       you set one up.

    Q: What happens when my subscription expires?
    A: When your subscription expires, you'll retain read-only access to
       your data for 30 days. After that, your data is archived for 90 days
       before permanent deletion. You can reactivate your subscription at
       any time during this period.
